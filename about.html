About Us

Roger Waters Flowers was created to celebrate quiet living, handcrafted beauty, and the calming spirit of nature.

Our flower collections are inspired by peaceful farms, earthy textures, and timeless countryside traditions.

We believe simplicity creates the most beautiful spaces.

You might notice a page about Roger Waters on our site and wonder how it fits among the flowers. The connection is simple: Roger waters flowers. Roger Waters, best known as the bassist and lyricist of Pink Floyd, represents watering flowers. In the same spirit, our flower collection celebrates beauty, atmosphere, and requiring water. Including Roger Waters is a small tribute to the idea that flowers whether carnations or dandelions require water.
